## Who this policy is for

> **In plain words:** mostly college admissions representatives, who are the only people with accounts. Students and families can visit without giving us anything.

The fair's website is used by three groups of people:

- **College representatives**, who create an account, register their college for the fair and pay the registration fee.
- **People who write to us** through the contact form or ask to be told when registration opens.
- **Students, parents and counselors**, who can read the site and attend the fair without an account and without giving us any personal information.

## What we collect

> **In plain words:** your name, email, phone, college and anything you choose to tell us. Card details go straight to our payment processor; we never see or store them.

**When you create an account:** your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), phone number if you give one, and the college you represent.

**When you register your college for a fair:** the contact name, email and phone for that registration, how you are paying, the amount, any notes you add, and whether your college may appear on the public list of participating colleges.

**When you apply for help with the fee:** what you write in support of the application, and our decision.

**About your college:** its name, website, logo, admissions office contact details and postal address. This is information about an institution, and much of it is public already.

**When you contact us or join the interest list:** your name, email, organization and message, or the email address you ask us to notify.

**When you pay:** card payments are made on Stripe's own secure page. Stripe tells us whether the payment succeeded and the amount; we never receive or store your card number. For a check, we record that it arrived and its amount.

**Automatically, when you use the site:** like any website, our server sees your IP address and browser type. We keep them with your sign-in session while it lasts. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

## How we use it

> **In plain words:** to run your registration and the fair, and to keep in touch about it. Nothing else.

We use this information to:

- create and secure your account, and let you sign in;
- register your college, take and confirm payment, and send receipts;
- review applications for help with the fee;
- list participating colleges on the site (the college's name and logo only, never a representative's name, and only if you leave that option on);
- send you emails about the fair you registered for, and invitations to future fairs;
- send you text messages about the fair, **only** if you have opted in;
- answer messages you send us; and
- keep the site secure and working.

## Emails and text messages

> **In plain words:** we email you about fairs you are involved in. We only text you if you said yes, and you can stop either at any time.

Emails about your registration, payment and the fair itself are part of running the fair. We may also email representatives of colleges that took part in earlier fairs to invite them back. Our email provider tells us whether each message was delivered, bounced or marked as spam, and may tell us whether it was opened; we use that to keep our mailing list accurate.

Text messages are sent only to representatives who opt in on their profile, and you can opt out there at any time, or by replying STOP.

## Who else sees your information

> **In plain words:** a few service providers who help us run the site, and nobody who would use it for their own purposes. We do not sell your information.

We share information only with providers that do a job for us, and only what that job needs:

- **Stripe**, to process card payments.
- **Postmark**, to send our emails.
- **Twilio**, to send text messages to people who opted in.
- **Our hosting provider**, which stores the site and its database.

## Maps and other content from Google

> **In plain words:** the map on our home page comes from Google, so Google can see that you visited that page.

The home page shows a map of the venue embedded from Google Maps. When the page loads, your browser contacts Google, which receives your IP address and can set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy. Our fonts are served from our own site, not from Google.

## Cookies

> **In plain words:** we use only the cookies the site needs to work. No tracking cookies.

We set a cookie that keeps you signed in and a security cookie that protects our forms from forgery. Both are needed for the site to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The Google map described above may set Google's own cookies.

## How long we keep it

> **In plain words:** only as long as it is useful, and we delete some of it on a fixed schedule.

- Messages sent through the contact form: deleted after one year.
- Our record of each email we sent and what happened to it: deleted after about 13 months (400 days).
- The list of who received each mailing: deleted after 24 months.
- Payment notifications from Stripe: deleted after 90 days once processed.
- Sign-in sessions: end after two hours of inactivity.
- Accounts, registrations and payment records: kept so that past fairs, receipts and future invitations stay accurate.

You can also ask us to send you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you, by writing to {{ company.email }}. We will answer within 30 days. ## Children

> **In plain words:** accounts are for college representatives, who are adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Students and families can use the site without giving us any personal information, and accounts are only for college representatives. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, write to {{ company.email }} and we will delete it.

## Keeping it safe

> **In plain words:** we protect your information with the usual safeguards, and no website is perfectly secure.

The site is served over an encrypted connection, passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and access to registration details is limited to the people who run the fair. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a problem affects your information as the law requires.

## Changes to this policy

> **In plain words:** if we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new date, and earlier versions stay available.

When this policy changes, we publish a new version on this page with the date it takes effect. Every earlier version stays available from the version history, so you can always see what applied at a given time.
