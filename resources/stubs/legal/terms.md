## Who can use the site

> **In plain words:** anyone can read it. Accounts are for people who represent a college and are allowed to register it.

Anyone may read the site. To create an account and register for the fair, you must be an adult who works for, or is authorised to act for, the college or university you register. A new representative's link to a college may need our approval before you can register it.

## Your account

> **In plain words:** keep your password private, keep your details current, and tell us if something looks wrong.

You are responsible for keeping your password private and for what happens under your account. Keep your contact details up to date so we can reach you about the fair. Tell us at {{ company.email }} if you think someone else has used your account. We may suspend an account that is used to break these terms.

## Registering for the fair

> **In plain words:** a registration is a place for your college at one fair. It is confirmed once it is paid for.

Each registration reserves a place for one college at one fair. A registration paid by card is confirmed when the payment succeeds. A registration paid by check holds your place from the moment you register and is confirmed when the check arrives. Places may be limited, and registration closes on the date shown for each fair.

## Fees and payment

> **In plain words:** the price you see when you register is the price you pay, even if the fee rises later.

The registration fee is shown before you commit. Some fairs have an early bird price that rises to a regular price on a stated date; your fee is set when you register, so a later price increase does not change what you owe. Card payments are processed by Stripe. Checks are made payable as shown on your registration form. Help with the fee may be available through an application in your account; an approved application changes the fee for that registration only.

## At the fair

> **In plain words:** be respectful, stay at your table, and follow the venue's rules.

Everyone at the fair agrees to the fair guidelines published in our FAQ: no recording or live streaming, no solicitation outside exhibitor tables, respectful behaviour towards everyone, following the event schedule, keeping aisles clear, and following the venue's rules. We may ask anyone who does not follow them to leave.

## Changes to the fair

> **In plain words:** dates, times or the venue can change. If they do, we will tell registered colleges as soon as we can.

We may need to change a fair's date, times or venue. If we do, we will tell registered representatives by email as soon as we reasonably can. What happens to a registration if a change means your college can no longer attend is covered under cancellations and refunds.
